First time here?

Start with these three things

ESM is an intentional community and a registered condominium at the same time. These pages explain both halves.

  1. Read our Collective Why and the Five Commitments

    The foundations of our community are purpose and commitments. Start with the Why statement neighbors wrote together, then the five commitments that give us a shared language for culture.

  2. Learn how decisions are made

    The Assembly of owners, the Owners Board and the Administrator each have a role. See who does what, meet the current Board, and learn how to vote on Loomio.

  3. Building? Follow the three-step guide

    Gather the official documents, design with permaculture and water in mind, then submit to the Architecture and Construction Committee.

Gatherings

We gather twice a year

Every February and August neighbors come together on the land to build connection and coherence, explore our emerging culture and governance, and get clarity on personal next steps for building and moving to ESM.

Next gathering: February 2027. Check the Gatherings page as the date approaches. The full program, neighbor offerings, menu and FAQ from the August 20–23, 2026 gathering are archived there.
