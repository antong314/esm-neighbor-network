ESM Common Area Usage Guidelines

Sharing our common spaces

At Ecovilla San Mateo, we aim for our shared spaces to be guided by personal responsibility, mutual respect and community care, rather than long lists of rules. These guidelines create a shared understanding of safety, coordination and shared enjoyment, recognizing that caring for common areas is part of caring for one another. This is a living system — "good enough for now, and safe enough to try." Last updated June 23, 2026 · source document ↗

Shared spaces remain shared

Common areas belong to everyone. Their use should reflect openness, respect and inclusion.

Coordination over control

Systems like reservations exist to help coordinate use — not to create exclusive usage.

Impact matters

Activities should be mindful of neighbors, shared resources, and the overall experience of the community.

Contribution to shared infrastructure

When common areas are used for outside-paying events, a portion supports the upkeep of shared spaces.

Common area use

Reservations

Residents may reserve common areas using the community app. A reservation communicates intended use (time, place, purpose), helps avoid overlap or confusion, and does not create exclusive or private control of the space. Even when reserved, common areas remain accessible to the community, neighbors should not be excluded, and reservations provide visibility, not exclusivity.

Hosting activities

Residents are welcome to host gatherings (birthdays, social events), classes, circles or workshops, and informal community activities — with the understanding that the space remains part of the shared life of the community.

Guests & participation

  • Residents may invite guests.
  • Activities in common areas should remain open to ESM neighbors, who may attend freely (donations may be appropriate in some cases).
  • If an event will be announced outside of ESM to invite non-community members, it must be hosted by an ESM owner.

Paying events

If the host or organizer is collecting revenue, 10% of gross revenue is contributed to the HOA. The organizer complies with all requirements set by the Administrator, which can include a security deposit, a pre-event site orientation, gate policies, returning the space clean and undamaged, and a post-event review.

Residents & guests

  • Overnight guests and renters are entitled to the same usage as owners and do not need to be accompanied by the owner.
  • Owners are asked to accompany transient or day visitors when using the pools and common areas.

Community pool guidelines

Hours & noise

  • Quiet hours: 8:00 PM – 8:00 AM. The pool remains open, but please minimize noise.

Safety & supervision

  • No lifeguard on duty — swim at your own risk.
  • Children under 12 must be supervised by an adult (18+).
  • Refrain from running, rough play, unsafe behavior or diving.

Health & hygiene

  • Shower before entering and remove soil from feet.
  • Proper swim attire required (no street clothes or nudity).
  • Do not use the pool with open wounds, infections or contagious illness.
  • Swim diapers required for infants and toddlers not toilet trained.

Cleanliness and prohibited items

  • Dispose of trash, recycle where possible, and leave the area clean. Do not remove pool furniture.
  • No pets in the pool area (except service animals where applicable). No smoking or vaping. No loud music or disruptive behavior. No large inflatables or items that limit shared use.
  • Do not tamper with equipment or safety devices; emergency equipment is for emergencies only.

Liability and contacts

The association is not responsible for lost or stolen items or injuries resulting from misuse. Maintenance requests: warner@laecovilla.com. For urgent situations, contact local emergency services.
