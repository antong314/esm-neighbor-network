Governance

How decisions are made

Ecovilla San Mateo is registered in Costa Rica as a condominium. Decision-making authority and responsibility is distributed across three groups: the Assembly of all owners, the Owners Board, and the Administrator.

The three bodiesOwners BoardAdministratorBylawsVoting on LoomioDues & budget

The Assembly

The governing body composed of all property owners. For all major decisions of the condominium, each lot — commercial and residential — is entitled to a single vote. Votes are held on Loomio, where each lot designates one delegate.

How to vote →

Board of Directors

The "Owners Board" is a representative body of seven neighbors chosen by the neighborhoods for a two-year term. The Board proposes the annual budget for Assembly approval and oversees the work of the Administrator.

Meet the Board →

The Administrator

The operational lead for daily management of the condominium, responsible for a small staff that maintains the common areas and infrastructure. Executes decisions from the Assembly and Board and escalates what needs higher-level approval.

Daily operations

The Administrator is the operational lead for daily management of the condominium, and is responsible for a small staff that maintains the common areas and infrastructure.

Responsibilities

  • Execute decisions from the Assembly and Board.
  • Handle accounts receivable and payable.

Process

Reglamento · Bylaws

The condominium bylaws

The Condominium and Administration Regulations govern relations within the condominium and are binding on all owners, residents, visitors, employees and suppliers. Any rental or use contract must reference them. Where these regulations are unclear, the Owners' Assembly provides the binding interpretation.

What's inside (67 pages)

  • General provisions, scope and definitions (Administrator, common areas, restricted-use areas, Assembly, Construction Committee, fees, Developer, and more)
  • Regulations on common property, including traffic, vehicles and parking, and leasing of lots
  • Security
  • The Assembly of condominium owners, the Board and the Administration
  • Fees, fines and dispute resolution

The project concept, as defined in the bylaws: a residential, commercial, educational and tourist condominium developed under a low-density concept in harmony with nature, using regenerative practices and seeking self-sustainability, efficiency and recycling, with the lowest possible impact and conservation of natural resources.

Voting platform

Making decisions on Loomio

We use Loomio for all major decisions that require a vote of the Assembly of owners, and for discussion within neighborhoods and project groups.

Go to Loomio ↗Loomio orientation slides ↗

Getting started

  1. Create an account and request to join

    Go to loomio.com/ecovilla-san-mateo and create an account if you don't already have one, then click Join.

  2. Name your lot's delegate

    Decide who will be the delegate for your lot, and include your lot ID and the selected delegate in your request. Only delegates may vote on key Assembly votes.

  3. Join your neighborhood group

    You may only join a neighborhood in which you have a lot.

  4. Personalize your profile

    Add a photo and description if you like.

How the groups are organized

GroupWho's in it
General AssemblyAll residents may participate in discussions and polls. Each lot selects one voter, marked as a Delegate. Only delegates vote on Condominium Assembly decisions.
Owners BoardRepresentatives selected by each neighborhood. They do not need to be a lot delegate.
Neighborhood subgroupsAlmendro, Bambú, Ceiba, Dalia, Espavel and Ficus. Includes all permanent residents of the neighborhood, with one or more admins. Neighborhoods may also create their own project subgroups.
Project subgroupsOne member is admin and coherence holder. Anyone called to participate is welcome to join.

Proposals, polls and outcomes

  • Threads. Every distinct proposal has its own thread under the Threads tab, with discussion and one or more polls. Proposals should be specific and actionable. During the initial launch, only group admins can create proposals and polls — work with an admin to get yours submitted.
  • Dashboard and Decisions. The Dashboard shows activity you might care about, including polls you haven't voted in. All polls, past and present, are under the Decisions tab so you can revisit any outcome and its discussion.
  • Poll types. From simple yes/no to fist-of-five to ranked choice. Depending on settings, results may be visible during voting or only after it closes. Some polls let you explain your vote.
  • Outcomes. Once a poll closes, its owner documents the outcome — a clear decision, or what new information or action is needed. Outcomes appear at the top of the poll for transparency.
