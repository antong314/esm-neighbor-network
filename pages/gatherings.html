Neighbor offerings

Sharing our gifts, building community

Neighbors offered these sessions during the gathering. Most ran in the neighborhood shalas and ranchos; see the program above for times and places.

Yoga for Body, Heart & Soul

Marcela Talavera · yoga teacher, ceremonialist, somatic movement facilitator

A mindful, multilevel practice to connect, breathe, move and come back to what truly matters: mindful movement, breathwork, presence and connection, deep relaxation. All levels welcome.

What Nobody Tells You Before Designing a House

Siulem Li Fallas · Architectural Feng Shui

Nine practical principles that integrate architecture, wellness and Classical Feng Shui to create spaces that promote harmony, relaxation and quality of life — whatever stage your project is at.

Movement, Meditation & Blessing for the Land

Lauren Jubelirer · licensed acupuncturist, healer, mentor, coach

A gentle movement and sacred dance experience that transitions into a Medicine for the Earth ceremony, offering gratitude, prayers and blessings for ESM and the greater Machuca Valley. No dance or meditation experience needed.

Ripple Effects Council

Keri & Jon Shinn · culture designers · bilingual session

A participatory council where we explore real-life situations and co-create wise, heart-centered responses — practical tools for building trust, navigating conflict with wisdom, and creating the kind of intentional culture that allows communities to truly flourish.

Cuban Salsa for Beginners

Juleana & Sebastian Walter

Salsa is an energetic, joyful and social Latin dance. Juleana and Sebastian, who taught together in Germany, show the basics of Cuban salsa in a playful way. Suitable for complete beginners.

Building in Costa Rica: An Open Conversation

Cata Elizondo Arieta · trained architect, runs a construction company

Ask anything about building in Costa Rica — legislation, regulations, services, project scope, materials and construction methods — in the context of a tropical, seismically active country.

Healthcare Providers Meet-up

All practitioners welcome

Connect, share and support: an opportunity for fellow practitioners to exchange ideas and build a supportive professional network for offering healthcare services in Costa Rica.

Trail Enthusiasts Meetup

Scott & Shara de Velder · trail stewards, Bambú

One of ESM's greatest treasures is its network of trails through more than 500 acres of protected forest, rivers and wildlife habitat. Discuss ways to enjoy, protect, improve and expand the trail system, and organize volunteer stewardship.

The Aliveness Practice: Living Fully

John "Hoppy" Hopkins · founder of Aliveness

An immersive experience on why we often drift from the lives we truly want, and how a conscious relationship with mortality can become a catalyst for clarity, courage and purpose.

Group Ear Acupuncture & Guided Meditation

Liz Roseman · licensed acupuncturist, personal trainer, life coach

Receive an ear acupuncture treatment in a group setting, followed by a short guided meditation. Possible benefits include pain relief and reduced stress and anxiety. Bring a yoga mat if you have one.

Ongo: Connection, Community & Purpose

Jim Jubelirer · business coach, energy healer

The Ongo Book 2.0: Everyday Nonviolence is a 24-week journey of weekly practices and one-on-one conversations that build trust and deeper relationships. Hear from past participants and learn about the next Ongo study group.

Breathwork Ceremony

Andrew Jenkin · certified breathwork facilitator

A one-hour breath journey designed to bring you to your edge in a safe, supportive way — leaving you more grounded, connected and expanded.

Voices for the Earth: A Community Song Circle

Colleen Porter · Song R'Evolutionist, sound & voice facilitator, violinist

Sing, connect, inspire, bless. Through simple songs and heartfelt harmonies we honor the land, the river and the air, and offer songs of gratitude as blessings for the Earth. Non-singers welcome.

Alignment Flow Yoga

Missy Graff Ballone · RYT500, movement educator, host of the Wellness for Makers podcast

Explore the steadiness of Hatha yoga, focusing on alignment within each pose, building strength and body awareness. Poses are held longer to develop kinesthetic awareness.

Women's Group & Men's Group Meet-ups

Thursday at 2:00 PM · open to all neighbors

Two groups, one community. A welcoming space for women of all ages and backgrounds, and a relaxed space for men to connect and support each other. Real conversations, real support, real growth.

AI: The Good, the Bad and the Ugly

Anton Gorshkov · AI start-up, communal deliberation tools

Explore the promise and the cautionary tale of AI — and how we choose to use technology so it strengthens rather than replaces communal thinking. Explore, reflect, decide together.

Qigong for Longevity

Jessica Fieo-Scully · Dipl.OM, L.Ac., co-founder of OmniQi

Ancient Qigong exercises you can do at home for self-healing, increased energy and longevity — strengthening the immune system, muscles, bones, tendons and organs.

Soul Science · Wave Research · Authentic Living

Adrián "Kuri" Badilla Chinca

A multidisciplinary space where meditation, tantra yoga, neuroscience, systems thinking, geometry, color, rhythm and wave energy are woven together to illustrate the intrinsic nature of reality. Some soft comedy at the end.

Frequently asked questions

Registration, meals and families

Answers from the August 2026 gathering FAQ. Most will apply to future gatherings too. Bilingual FAQ document ↗

Registration

How do I register for the Gathering?

A registration form is published in English and Spanish. Payment must be completed before filling out the form, because you will need to enter the authorization number from your payment confirmation email.

Can I visit the community during the Gathering without registering?

Absolutely. The Gathering doesn't take over the whole community — you're always welcome to enjoy the pools, trails and common areas and visit with friends, registered or not. Registration is specifically for the scheduled sessions, workshops, meals and other planned activities, since those costs are covered by registration fees. Participants wear name tags. During meal times, seating is arranged for registered participants, so if you're visiting during a meal period please enjoy the common areas rather than the dining seating.

Do I have to purchase the meal plan to attend?

No. You may choose the Registration Only option and bring your own meals or dine elsewhere. All talks, workshops and community activities are included with registration.

Will I need a ticket or name tag for meals?

Yes. Each registered participant receives personalized tickets for each meal in their plan, plus a name tag. Parents or guardians may hold and present tickets and name tags for their children.

Children & families

Are children's meals available? What ages qualify?

Yes. Discounted meal packages are available for children 13 and under; children under 6 are free. So the catering team can prepare enough food, please register your child rather than sharing your meal.

Meals & dietary needs

What meals are included in the meal package?

Lunch and dinner on all three days of the Gathering. Water is available; additional beverages may be offered depending on the menu.

Can I buy meals for only one day, or add the plan later?

The meal plan is offered as a package covering all lunches and dinners. Once the catering deadline has passed, menu preference changes cannot be guaranteed, so sign up early.

What food options are available? Can allergies be accommodated?

You choose vegan, vegetarian or omnivore when registering so the catering team can plan quantities. List any allergies or medical dietary restrictions on your registration form; the team will do its best but cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

Can I bring my own food? Is there refrigeration?

Absolutely — many participants bring snacks or packed meals. Limited refrigeration may be available in the neighbor ranchos, but plan to be responsible for storing your own food.

Can I invite non-registered guests to join meals?

Meals are reserved for registered participants who purchased the meal package. Contact the organizers in advance if you have a special circumstance.
