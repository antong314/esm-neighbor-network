Payment FAQ

Common questions

Payments & timing

Can I pay several months in advance?

Yes — you can pay any number of months at once. Always include the lot number and months covered in the payment reference.

Can I pay before the 1st of the month?

Yes, payment can be made at any time.

Is there a discount for paying the full year upfront?

No. Early payment is convenient for reducing international wire fees, but no discount is offered. The monthly budget does not allow for a shortfall.

Can I set up automatic payments?

Please consult your bank about scheduling recurring transfers.

I own multiple lots. Can I bundle them into one payment?

Yes. Clearly specify each lot number and the months you are covering in the payment reference. This is important for accurate record-keeping.

Ownership

I am reselling my lot, or haven't taken possession of my title yet. Am I still responsible for dues?

Yes, in both cases.

Payment methods & bank details

Can I pay in Canadian dollars or another currency?

No — payments must be made in US dollars or Costa Rican colones.

Can I pay via WISE?

Transfers can be sent through WISE, but the condominium does not have a WISE account. Use WISE to send funds to the BAC account details above.

What does "direct transfer" mean?

An online transfer from your bank account to the ESM account using the banking details in this guide.

The account name looks unusual. Is it correct?

Yes. That is the full legal name of the condominium. If your bank's field won't fit the full name, fill in as much as possible, starting from the beginning.

I bank with Banco Nacional. Can I transfer from there?

Yes, you can initiate a transfer from Banco Nacional to the BAC account.

Does ESM have an official phone number?

Not currently. Contact esmadmin@laecovilla.com.

Who manages the money

The condominium operates through an administration company that holds the bank account, separate from the developer. The Board drafts the budget, the Assembly approves it, and the Administrator handles accounts receivable and payable. Payment notifications are sent from QuickBooks by email.
