Phase I

Is this saving? That's amazing, we don't need WIX anymore! ?! here is a title: The first six neighborhoods

Phase I encompasses the initial six neighborhoods — Almendro, Bambú, Ceiba, Dalia, Espavel and Ficus — their common areas, and the sports area. All major features have been completed and handed over, with some exceptions based on issues that were uncovered during handover.

The Owners Board has formed a small focus team to work with the developer on the Phase 1 transition: documentation, legal framework, and testing the infrastructure through a full rainy season before final acceptance.

The Ecovilla development company continues to hold monthly update calls on Wednesdays, which you can find on the community calendar. Recordings and summaries for previous calls are archived below.

Town Center · Phase 2

Draft conceptual master plan for the expansion

In May 2026 the developer acquired 22 acres of front land and began planning a new neighborhood and town center. These are very early rough drafts. Nothing has been decided, and there is potential for mixing and matching aspects of the different options. The plan below is revision 3, June 2026, prepared for Marcelo Valansi by VIDA Masterplanning + Design.

72new lots in Guarumo I (34) and Guarumo II (38)
42apartments: 30 in the village, 12 turn-key
300students in the expanded Real World School
20turn-key Los Abuelos homes, 200 m² each
What's in the draft plan (26 numbered elements)

All amenities open to guests on the west side of the Machuca River; expansion of the existing school; new village and art village; relocation of the event venue; new sports area and Los Abuelos homes; new Guarumo neighborhood.

  1. Main arrival to Ecovilla San Mateo
  2. Village with commercial spaces, office & co-working spaces, art venues & workshops (60–100 m² each) and youth hub, with apartments on the upper floors: 4 art spaces, 7 commercial spaces, 5 office spaces, 3 food & beverage destinations, 30 apartments (60–120 m²)
  3. Parking area for the village and events
  4. Event venue (relocated structure)
  5. Backstage & event operations arrival gate to Guarumo neighborhood
  6. Green corridor with stormwater creek
  7. Wellness hub
  8. 12 turn-key apartments (90 m² each)
  9. Access to the school
  10. Parking area of the school
  11. Existing Real World School
  12. Expansion of the Real World School for 300 students in total
  13. Soccer field for school
