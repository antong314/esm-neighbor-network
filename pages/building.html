Step 2

Design your home

Resources to help you design a home that harmonizes with nature — and with the water and waste systems the community relies on.

Permaculture design

Learn more about permaculture and how to think about building on your lot in a manner that harmonizes with nature. Yam's introduction walks through the ethics and twelve principles:

  • Observe and interact
  • Catch and store energy — slow, spread, sink and store water
  • Obtain a yield
  • Apply self-regulation and accept feedback
  • Use and value renewable resources and services
  • Produce no waste
  • Design from patterns to details
  • Integrate rather than segregate
  • Use small and slow solutions
  • Use and value diversity
  • Use edges and value the marginal
  • Creatively use and value change

"Permaculture is not about what we do, it's about how we do things." — Dr. Ruben Arbib

Step 3

Submit your designs to the CAC

The ESM Architecture and Construction Committee (CAC, Comité de Arquitectura y Construcción) is the technical body appointed by the Owners' Assembly. It interprets the construction rules, reviews every construction proposal, and ensures the architectural harmony established by the project developer. These documents outline the process for obtaining approval from the CAC as well as the relevant national and municipal permitting processes.
